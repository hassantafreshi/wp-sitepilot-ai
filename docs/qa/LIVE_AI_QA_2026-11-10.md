# Live AI QA — Form Studio and the Easy Form Builder copilot (2026-11-10)

Site: demo.whitestudio.team (WordPress 7.1.4, Easy Form Builder 4.3.1 Pro; Conditional Logic, Stripe, PayPal and ZarinPal add-ons; the AI provider configured on the demo). Plugin: AI Site Assistant AutoPilot 0.0.1, branch `claude/kind-hamilton-ihprus`. Harness: `tests/live_ai_scenarios.cjs` (each scenario is a real request to the real AI through the plugin's REST API, then a check of the answer). Scenarios come from the feature inventory in `docs/research/AI_FORM_BUILDER_FEATURE_INVENTORY_2026-11.md`.

**Run 1 (before fixes):** 198 of 200 passed. **Run 2 (after fixes, two scenarios added):** 202 of 202 passed.

Run 1 failures and the defects found by reading every answer (not only the pass/fail checks) are published as GitHub issues #2–#17 in `hassantafreshi/wp-sitepilot-ai`: nine fixed and closed with their commits, seven open (features and gaps).

Plan limits that cannot be switched on the live site without changing its licence (Free, Free Plus) are covered by local WordPress tests with canned AI answers: `tests/wordpress_efb_logic_integration.php` (12 checks), `tests/wordpress_efb_payment_integration.php` (15) and `tests/wordpress_form_studio_layout_integration.php` (12).
